# Coffee Roots — Photography Brief

> Transcribed from the client brief (PDF). Deliverable lives in
> [IMAGE_PROMPTS.md](IMAGE_PROMPTS.md). Constraints from SPEC.md §0 and §10
> (no text/logos/signage, generic/unbranded, not depicting the real
> business) apply on top of everything below.

The Coffee Roots website is nearly complete. Replace every placeholder image
with original AI-generated photography using Higgsfield AI. Act as an Art
Director and Commercial Photographer. Study the entire website (colors,
typography, layout, animations, branding, mood, and business identity)
before creating prompts. The images must feel like they belong to one
premium brand, not random stock photos.

## Creative Direction

Business: COFFEE ROOTS — Specialty Coffee Shop, Almaty, Kazakhstan

Style: Scandinavian minimalism · warm natural wood · cozy premium café ·
editorial lifestyle photography · luxury coffee branding · natural daylight ·
soft shadows · earth-tone palette · modern architecture · calm atmosphere ·
clean composition · high-end commercial photography.

The photography should feel comparable to premium specialty coffee brands.

Avoid: cartoon, CGI, unrealistic lighting, oversaturated colors,
artificial-looking people, generic stock photo compositions, text inside
images, watermarks, logos.

## Technical Requirements

Every prompt specifies: photorealistic, ultra high resolution, commercial
advertising quality, editorial photography, natural lighting, 35mm or 50mm
lens when appropriate, realistic depth of field, clean composition, soft
color grading, warm color temperature, premium coffee branding aesthetic.

Each image includes: camera angle, lens suggestion, lighting, composition,
mood, color palette, subject description, negative prompt.

Prompts are returned in a structured format for direct copy into Higgsfield AI.
